Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • The app does not require user registration.
  • Any images captured or edited within the app remain on your device and are not uploaded to external servers.

Automatically collected information

  • The app may collect usage data, such as how many times the camera button is pressed, to determine when to show ads.
  • We collect anonymized analytics data to improve the app's performance and user experience.
2

How We Use Information

  • Provide camera and gallery functionalities.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Display advertisements to generate revenue.
  • Show a rate app prompt for feedback and reviews.
  • Provide and manage optional Pro subscription features.
3

Permissions We Request

  • Camera permission — required for taking photos. Only requested when you access the camera.
  • Read media images permission — required to access and display images in the app's gallery.
  • Internet and network access permissions — required for displaying ads, checking internet connectivity, and processing subscription purchases.
4

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads (banner and interstitial). AdMob uses the following mediation partners to help serve ads, who may also collect anonymous usage data for ad personalization:

  • InMobi
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook)

Refer to Google's Advertising Policies, InMobi's Privacy Policy, and Meta's Privacy Policy for more details.

5

Subscriptions and Payments

Real Mirror offers an optional auto-renewing subscription ("Real Mirror Pro") that removes ads and unlocks additional features.

All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, collect, or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other payment information — Google handles this directly.

The app stores only whether your account currently has an active Pro subscription, cached on your device, so features can be unlocked without checking online every time.

Subscription price, billing period, and auto-renewal terms are shown before purchase and are also available in the Google Play Store.

Managing your subscription: subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date. You can manage or cancel anytime from Google Play's subscription settings, also linked directly from the app's Settings screen. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies, not ours directly.
6

Face Detection and Analysis

The app uses ML Kit Face Detection to detect face angles and provide user feedback.

No facial data is stored, transmitted, or shared with third parties.

Refer to ML Kit Terms of Service for more details.
