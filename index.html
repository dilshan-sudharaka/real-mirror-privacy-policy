Overview

Real Mirror is a mirror and selfie camera with optional face-analysis tools (Face Shape and Face Angle). This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information.

  • No account. You don't sign up, log in or give us your name or email address to use the app.
  • Your photos and face scans stay on your device. We have no servers that receive them, and the app never uploads them.
  • Face analysis happens on your phone. The camera image is analysed in real time on the device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Ads, ad measurement and payments involve partners. Free users see ads served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. Payments for Real Mirror Pro are handled entirely by Google Play. Where it is allowed (it is not used in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), a TikTok measurement service tells us whether our TikTok ads led to installs and purchases (see section 7). We do not deliberately send it your photos, face scans, face-shape results, face measurements or gender.
  • You stay in control. You can change your answers, manage ad choices, delete photos and cancel a subscription at any time (see section 11).

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • The app does not require user registration, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Gender choice (Male or Female). When you first use Face Shape the app asks which of the two you'd like, so it can show style suggestions that suit you. Answering is your choice: you can close the question without answering, and you can change your answer later in Settings. The answer is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Face Shape photo permission. Your yes/no answer to the Face Shape photo question (see section 3) is stored on your device.
  • Photos you take with the camera are stored on your device (see section 4). We do not receive them.

Information collected automatically

  • Counters kept on your device. The app keeps simple counters, such as how many times you tapped the camera button or opened the app, to decide when to show an ad or the Pro offer. They never leave your device through us.
  • Subscription status. A single yes/no value for whether your Google Play account has an active Pro subscription, cached on your device so features unlock without a network check every time.
  • Information collected by our ad and Google partners. When ads are shown or a purchase is made, Google and the ad partners listed in sections 7 and 9 may collect technical data such as your advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system details, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive that data.
  • Ad-campaign measurement (TikTok). We advertise Real Mirror on TikTok. Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, TikTok's App Events SDK runs inside the app to attribute installs to our TikTok ads, to understand app activity after the install, and to measure how our advertising performs. It sends TikTok: app activity (that the app was installed and opened for the first time, that it was opened, that it was opened again the next day, and a bare count each time a Face Shape scan finishes with a result); purchase information (if you buy Pro, the price and currency of the plan, but not your payment details, purchase token or Google account); and device and technical identifiers (your advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, operating-system and app version, language, screen size, network type, the app store that installed the app, the Google Play "install referrer" that shows which ad or link led to the install, and random IDs the SDK creates for the installation and for each session). We do not deliberately send TikTok your photos, face shape, face measurements or gender, or your name, email address or phone number. We do not receive this data ourselves; TikTok shows us totals. Section 7 explains when the SDK runs and what your choices do.
  • We do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting service and we have no servers that collect your usage data. The only measurement service in the app is the TikTok one described above, and it sends its data to TikTok, not to us.

Face Features: Face Shape, Face Angle and Real Face

The app uses Google ML Kit Face Detection and ML Kit Face Mesh to find a face in the camera image, to read its outline and head angle, and to draw guides on the screen. This analysis runs on your device, on the live camera image.

The measurements taken from your face (for example the proportions that decide your face shape, or the angle of your head) are used on the spot to show you a result. They are not stored, not uploaded and not shared, and they are gone when you close the result or leave the camera.

Face Shape: comparison photo (required for this feature)

To use Face Shape, you must agree to let the app automatically take one photo of your face during each scan. The photo is shown next to your detected face-shape outline so you can compare them. This photo:

  • Is captured and processed entirely on your device.
  • Is never uploaded, transmitted or shared with us or any third party.
  • Is held only in your phone's memory while the result is on the screen and is discarded when you close it. It is never saved to your storage or photo gallery.

You are asked every time Face Shape is opened without your agreement. If you choose "Not now", Face Shape is simply not available — the rest of the app keeps working. You can change your answer at any time in Settings.

Face Shape: gender and style suggestions

The Face Shape result includes style suggestions (hairstyles, eyebrows, glasses, earrings, beard and necklines). The pictures used for them are built into the app. The app picks them on your device from your detected face shape and the Male/Female choice you made. Your face shape, your choice, your comparison photo and your face measurements are never sent anywhere. The one thing about Face Shape that can leave your device is a bare "a Face Shape scan was completed" count, sent to TikTok for our advertising measurement where that runs (not in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland; see section 7), without the result.

Face Angle

Face Angle uses the live camera image to show how level your head is and to give on-screen guidance. It does not take or store a photo.

Real Face (mirror and selfie camera)

The front-camera preview may apply a light skin-smoothing effect. It is rendered on your device in real time, and the original camera data is not sent anywhere.

Refer to the ML Kit Terms of Service for more details.

Not medical or identity technology. Face Shape and Face Angle are for style and fun. The app does not identify who you are, does not recognise people, and does not make health or medical judgements.

Photos You Take

  • Photos you capture with the camera are saved on your device in the Pictures / Real Mirror folder, where your gallery app can also see them.
  • The app's own gallery shows only the photos in that folder. You can view them, flip them (which saves a mirrored copy) or delete them.
  • Photos are never uploaded by the app. If your phone's own backup or a gallery/cloud app (for example Google Photos) backs up your pictures, that is governed by your settings in that service and not by Real Mirror.
  • Photos stay on your device until you delete them.

Permissions We Request

  • Camera — required for the mirror preview, taking photos and the face features. Asked for only when you open the camera.
  • Photos and media (read images) — on Android 13 and newer "Read media images", on Android 12 and older "Read external storage". Asked for only when you open the in-app gallery or view your latest photo, to list, show and delete the photos Real Mirror saved in its folder.
  • Internet and network state — required for showing ads, the ad-consent form, Google Play purchases, update checks, ad-campaign measurement, downloading face-detection components through Google Play services, and checking whether you are online.
  • Google Play install referrer and advertising ID — the app reads these through Google Play and Google Play services (no permission prompt) for ad delivery and, where the TikTok SDK runs (section 7), for TikTok's campaign measurement.

The app does not request access to your microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone or messages, and it does not record video or audio. You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

Advertising and Ad Consent

The free version shows ads (banner and full-screen "interstitial" ads) served by Google AdMob. AdMob uses the following mediation partners to help serve ads. They may also collect data such as your advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads:

  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook)
  • Unity Ads

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your choices. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and your choice decides whether ads are requested and how they are personalised. If you change your mind, use Settings → Privacy Options (shown where required) to review your choice. In US states where a privacy law lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data, the same entry opens our US privacy-choices message (see "US state privacy choices and Limited Data Use" below). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's own settings.

The Real Mirror Pro subscription removes ads. Pro users don't see banner or full-screen ads.

Refer to Google's Advertising Policies, Meta's Privacy Policy and Unity's Privacy Policy for more details.

Measuring our TikTok advertising

We advertise Real Mirror on TikTok. To attribute installs to those ads, to understand app activity after the install, and to measure how our advertising performs (installs, app opens, Face Shape scans and Pro purchases), the app includes TikTok's App Events SDK (provided by TikTok and its group companies). What it sends is listed in section 2. The app decides whether the SDK may run from the region and the privacy choices that Google's consent platform reports for your phone:

  • EEA, the UK and Switzerland: switched off. Where Google's consent platform reports that the GDPR applies to you, the TikTok SDK is not started, whatever you answer in the ad-consent form, and the SDK sends nothing to TikTok. The SDK is also not started until the consent platform has worked out which rules apply to you; if that cannot be determined when the app opens (for example because you are offline), the SDK is not started at that point. This follows Google's determination of your region, so it cannot cover a case where that determination is wrong or unavailable. If the rules that apply to you change while the app is open (for example you travel into one of these regions), the SDK is stopped for the rest of that session and the events it had queued are deleted.
  • US states with a privacy-choices message: your choice applies. Where our US privacy-choices message applies to you, you decide in Settings → Privacy Options, and the SDK runs in TikTok's Limited Data Use mode unless you have chosen to allow the sale or sharing of your data (details below).
  • Everywhere else: the SDK runs without a consent form. The app has no in-app switch for it there. You can limit it by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings (the SDK also uses other identifiers listed in section 2, so this does not stop it from sending events), or end it by uninstalling the app.
  • We switched off the SDK's optional features that record taps, button texts and screen contents, that log Google Play purchases automatically, and that send the SDK's own health metrics. The SDK is TikTok's software and some of its settings are controlled from TikTok's servers, so beyond the features we can switch off and what the app itself passes to it, we cannot inspect or control what it does.

US state privacy choices and Limited Data Use

In US states with a privacy law that lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data, Google's consent platform shows our privacy-choices message. Open it at any time from Settings → Privacy Options (the entry appears only where the message applies to you). The message is called "My data preferences" and offers two choices:

  • Don't sell or share my data. The app runs the TikTok SDK in Limited Data Use mode. Every event the SDK sends is marked as "limited data use", which limits how TikTok may use it under TikTok's terms and applicable law. This takes effect straight away, including while the app is open.
  • Allow the sale or sharing of my data. The SDK runs without that restriction. If you switch from "Don't sell or share" back to "Allow", the restriction is lifted the next time you open the app, not while it is running.
  • No choice saved yet. Until you open Privacy Options and save a choice, the app cannot tell whether you opted out, so as a cautious default it runs the SDK in Limited Data Use mode for you.

What this does not do. Limited Data Use is a restriction on how TikTok may use the data, set by TikTok; it is not a promise that nothing is sent. The app still contacts TikTok and still sends the events and identifiers listed in section 2, marked as limited data use. Events sent before you changed your choice have already reached TikTok and cannot be recalled by us; please contact TikTok about data it holds. The app reads your choice from the signals Google's consent platform stores on your phone. If that choice cannot be read (for example the signal is missing, incomplete or in a format the app does not recognise), the app treats it as restricted, not as consent. Where no US privacy-choices message applies to you, there is no in-app choice for the TikTok SDK (see "Everywhere else" above). The same Privacy Options choice is also used by Google for the ads in the app; how Google and the mediation partners above handle it is described in their own policies.

Refer to TikTok's Privacy Policy for how TikTok handles the data it receives.

Subscriptions and Payments

Real Mirror offers an optional auto-renewing subscription ("Real Mirror Pro") that removes ads and unlocks additional features such as the Face Shape style tips in full and the Face Angle detector.

All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, collect, or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other payment information — Google handles this directly.

The app stores only whether your account currently has an active Pro subscription, cached on your device, so features can be unlocked without checking online every time. "Restore Purchases" asks Google Play whether your account has an active purchase.

Subscription price, billing period, and auto-renewal terms are shown before purchase and are also available in the Google Play Store.

Managing your subscription: subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date. You can manage or cancel anytime from Google Play's subscription settings, also linked directly from the app's Settings screen. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies, not ours directly.

Data on Your Device, Retention and Backup

We do not store personal data on our own servers, because we have none that receive your data. What the app keeps on your device:

  • Photos you took, in Pictures / Real Mirror — until you delete them.
  • Your Male/Female choice and your answer to the Face Shape photo question — until you change them or clear the app's data.
  • Your cached Pro status and the ad / app-open counters — until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • If the TikTok SDK runs: random IDs and an "installed" marker created by the SDK, and events waiting to be sent — until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. The events waiting to be sent are also deleted if the SDK is stopped (for example because the GDPR applies to you). These files are excluded from backup.
  • Nothing from your face scans is kept: no face measurements, no comparison photo, no scan history.

Backup. Android's automatic backup may include some of the app's small settings (such as your Male/Female choice and the counters) in your Google account backup, according to your phone's backup settings. Your Face Shape photo answer and your Pro status cache are excluded from backup, so on a new phone you are asked again and Pro status is re-checked with Google Play.

Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone's Settings removes everything the app stored internally. Photos in your gallery stay until you delete them.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Gender choice — change it any time in Settings.
  • Face Shape photo permission — review or change it any time in Settings. Declining turns Face Shape off; it does not affect other features.
  • Ad choices — answer or change the ad-consent form (Settings → Privacy Options where shown). You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.
  • TikTok measurement — not used in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. In US states where our privacy-choices message applies, choose "Don't sell or share my data" in Settings → Privacy Options to run it in Limited Data Use mode (section 7). Elsewhere there is no in-app switch for it: you can limit it by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or end it by uninstalling the app.
  • Permissions — turn Camera or Photos access off in your phone's Settings at any time (the related features then stop working).
  • Photos — delete them in the app's gallery or in your gallery app.
  • Subscription — manage or cancel in Google Play.

Because we don't hold personal data about you on our own systems, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California) you may have rights over the data our ad and Google partners hold about you; please use the partners' tools and privacy policies linked above, or contact us and we will point you in the right direction. We do not sell your personal information for money. Passing data to advertising and measurement partners (section 7) can count as "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising under some laws, such as California's. Where our privacy-choices message applies to you, you can opt out in Settings → Privacy Options: the app then runs the TikTok SDK in Limited Data Use mode. That limits how the data may be used but does not stop the app from sending it, and it does not recall data already sent (see section 7).

Security

  • Face analysis, comparison photos and your photo library stay on your device, which keeps them out of reach of our systems altogether.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play, and the app never sees your payment details.
  • Network connections used by the app and its partner services are made over the standard secure channels those services provide.
  • No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your phone updated and locked helps protect your photos.

Children's Privacy

Real Mirror is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app or has provided information through it, contact us and we will help. Our advertising and measurement partners (Google, TikTok, Meta and Unity) process data under their own policies, so we may also point you to them.

Ages 13 to 17. The app does not ask for your age and does not use your age to decide whether ads or the TikTok SDK run. We therefore cannot promise that data from users aged 13 to 17 is withheld from advertising or measurement partners. What protects you depends on where you are and on your choices, not on your age: the TikTok SDK is switched off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; where our US privacy-choices message applies it runs in Limited Data Use mode unless you choose to allow the sale or sharing of your data; and in other places it runs without a consent form (section 7). In some countries a higher age applies for agreeing to the use of data for advertising and measurement; if that is the case where you live and you are younger than that age, please ask a parent or guardian before you answer the consent form or change your privacy choices. A parent can also limit this by resetting the advertising ID in the phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.
